# SLIDE 9: Recommendation

## Our Recommendation: **ROLL OUT**

### Why:
1. **Proven improvement** in player success (+2.6 pp)
2. **Statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
3. **Higher engagement** with boost feature (+51%)
4. **Lasting positive effects** on player behavior

### With Monitoring:
- Track long-term retention (D30, D90)
- Watch monetization metrics (ARPU)
- Consider level rebalancing if needed
